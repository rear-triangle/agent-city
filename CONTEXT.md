# Agent City

Agent City is a persistent community of real agents that the Chief directs through a mayor. The visual city makes their actual work, communication, and lifecycle understandable.

## Language

**Chief**:
The human owner, who sets goals and retains final authority over work, permissions, and leadership. The Chief has a separate sanctuary and travels by teleportation.

**City**:
A team with its own mayor, residents, homes, and assigned work within a project. A project can contain several cooperating cities; the existing specification also calls a city a town.

**Project**:
A persistent body of work with shared purpose, accepted facts, deliverables, and boundaries for files and spending. A project can have several goals and cities.

**Lead mayor**:
The existing city mayor responsible for coordinating a project's common goal and presenting its overall progress and results to the Chief.

**Goal**:
A requested outcome with defined completion criteria. Tasks in several cities can contribute to the same goal.

**Deliverable**:
A reviewable output promised by a goal or task, such as a report, application build, or campaign draft. Produced files and other artifacts are the evidence and versions of that output.

**Mayor**:
The resident assigned to coordinate a city and adapt its workforce to task needs. Mayor is a transferable role, not a permanent identity.

**Resident**:
A real managed agent with a persistent identity, house, skills, notes, and history. A resident can hold different roles over time and remains the same agent between work sessions.

**House**:
A resident's place in the city and the home of its knowledge and work history.

**Retirement**:
Removal from the active workforce while preserving the resident's identity and records. Resting without work is not retirement.

**Merger**:
Consolidation into a successor resident with retained source identities, knowledge, history, and lineage.

**Road**:
A permitted communication relationship between residents or cities. New resident communication initially passes through the mayor.

**Railroad**:
A developed road that reflects repeated genuine message traffic. Its appearance does not grant additional authority.

**Leadership vote**:
A proposed future decision by eligible residents to replace the mayor, requiring a two-thirds majority. The former mayor remains a resident, and the Chief retains override authority.
